DECCAN MOTOR FINANCE
Car Loan Key Terms

Loan amount: Rs. 6,50,000
Interest rate: 9.5% p.a. (fixed)
Tenure: 60 months

1. Foreclosure charges are 6% of the principal outstanding if the loan is closed within 12 months of disbursal, and 4% thereafter.
2. Part-prepayment is not allowed in the first year.
3. Vehicle insurance must be bought through the Lender's nominated insurer, and the first-year premium of Rs. 32,000 shall be added to the loan amount.
4. Documentation charges of Rs. 2,500 are payable upfront.
5. If the NACH mandate is dishonoured, a charge of Rs. 750 plus GST will be levied per instance.
6. Overdue EMIs will attract penal charges at 24% per annum on the overdue amount.
7. The vehicle will be hypothecated to the Lender until the loan is fully repaid.
8. The Lender will issue a No Objection Certificate within 15 days of full repayment.
