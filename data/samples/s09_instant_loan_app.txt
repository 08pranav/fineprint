PAISABOLT - Instant Credit Line Agreement

Credit line: Rs 25,000. Each withdrawal is repayable in 62 days.

a) The amount credited to your bank account is net of a platform fee of 4% of the withdrawal, along with GST.
b) Interest is charged at 0.1% per day on the amount withdrawn.
c) For every day of delay beyond the due date, a delay fee of Rs 50 per day is added to your dues.
d) By signing up, you permit the PaisaBolt app to read your SMS inbox and your contact list for credit assessment and for collection purposes.
e) Your credit line may be suspended or reduced at the absolute discretion of the Company.
f) Any amount due may be recovered from any bank account linked to your UPI AutoPay mandate.
g) You may repay early at any time; no fee applies for early repayment.
h) Grievances can be raised with the Grievance Redressal Officer at the address in the app.
