SURAKSHA GENERAL INSURANCE CO.
Family Health Shield - Policy Schedule and Key Terms

Policy No: HS2290045871
Sum Insured: ₹5,00,000 (Family floater)
Annual Premium: ₹14,850 including GST

Waiting Periods
1. Initial waiting period: Claims for any illness in the first 30 days of the policy are not payable, except accidents.
2. Pre-existing diseases are covered only after a waiting period of 36 months of continuous coverage.
3. Specified illnesses such as cataract, hernia and joint replacement have a waiting period of 24 months.

Cost Sharing
4. A co-payment of 20% applies to every claim when the insured member is above 60 years of age.
5. Room rent is limited to 1% of the sum insured per day. If a higher room is chosen, all associated expenses will be paid proportionately.

Exclusions
6. Cosmetic surgery, dental treatment and infertility treatment are permanently excluded.

Renewal and Cancellation
7. This policy will be renewed automatically every year and the premium will be debited from your registered account unless you opt out at least 15 days before the renewal date.
8. Free-look period: You may cancel the policy within 30 days of receipt and get a refund of the premium, less a proportionate deduction for the period of cover.
9. Cashless claims will be authorised within one hour of the request from a network hospital.
