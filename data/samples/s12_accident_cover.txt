SHIELD PERSONAL ACCIDENT COVER - Certificate Terms

Sum insured: Rs. 10,00,000
Premium: Rs. 1,999 per annum plus GST

1. This cover renews each year on the policy anniversary through the e-mandate registered at enrolment.
2. Claims arising within the first 90 days from the start of cover shall not be admissible, except for accidental death.
3. A deductible of Rs. 5,000 applies to each claim for medical expenses and is borne by the insured.
4. The insurer may revise premium rates at renewal with the approval of the regulator.
5. Claims must be reported within 30 days of the accident with the documents listed in Annexure A.
