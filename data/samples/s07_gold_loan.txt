SUVARNA GOLD LOANS PVT LTD
Gold Loan Pledge Form - Terms

Loan amount: ₹1,20,000 against 32 grams of gold ornaments.
Rate of interest: 18% per annum, payable monthly.
Tenure: 12 months

1. If interest is not paid within 90 days, interest will be charged at 24% per annum from the date of disbursal on the full loan amount.
2. A processing fee of ₹500 is payable at the time of the loan.
3. The loan may be closed at any time. If it is closed within 7 days, interest for a minimum of 7 days will be charged.
4. The Company shall have a general lien on all ornaments pledged by the Borrower under any loan account, and may adjust the sale proceeds against dues in any other loan of the Borrower.
5. If dues remain unpaid after the tenure, the ornaments may be auctioned after 14 days' written notice to the Borrower.
6. The Borrower will receive a pledge receipt showing the weight and purity of the ornaments.
