METRO BANK SIGNATURE CREDIT CARD - Schedule of Charges

Annual Membership Fee: Rs. 2,500 + taxes, reversed on annual spends of Rs. 3 lakh.
Total credit limit: Rs. 2,00,000

(i) The interest-free period of up to 50 days is not available if any previous balance is carried forward. Finance charges of 3.6% p.m. then apply on all transactions from the transaction date.
(ii) Paying just the Minimum Amount Due will keep your card in good standing, but the remaining balance is revolved with finance charges.
(iii) Late Payment Fee: Rs. 1,200 if the total amount due exceeds Rs. 25,000.
(iv) The Bank may share card member data with its co-brand partner for rewards and promotional communication.
(v) The Bank shall be entitled to set off any outstanding on the card against any deposit of the card member with the Bank.
(vi) Fuel surcharge of 1% is waived on transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000.
